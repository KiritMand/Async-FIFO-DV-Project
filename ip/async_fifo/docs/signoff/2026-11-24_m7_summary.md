# async_fifo — M7 formal sign-off summary (2026-11-24)

Committed copy of `out/async_fifo/formal/signoff.md` (written by `common/scripts/formal_run.py`).
The SBY work directories, logs and traces stay in `out/async_fifo/formal/`.

- **Scope.** This is the **SO-3 run** (vplan §7). k-induction (`prove`) and every cover (`cover`)
  at ASIZE 2 and 3, DSIZE 4, with a bounded check (`bmc`, depth 40) and ABC PDR (`pdr`) as
  cross-checks. Then the assumption audit (`audit_a01`, `audit_a02`, `audit_a05`) and the
  no-invariant cross-checks (`prove_noinv`, `pdr_noinv`). Each run's verdict is judged against an
  expectation: `pass`, `fail` (an audit must fail) or `any` (recorded).
- **What is proven.** All 25 spec assertions of vplan §4 (Runs = formal or both), plus the 25
  `inv_` induction invariants, hold for every input sequence and every interleaving of the two
  clocks, coincident edges included. All 22 covers are reached, so the assumptions are not
  vacuous. Assumptions: A01, A02 and A05 (A03 not adopted).
- **Source.** git `d10d9d7` plus the M7 changes in the working tree, committed as the M7 commit.
  Toolchain: OSS CAD Suite 2026-11-21 (Yosys 0.71+77, SBY 0.71), yices via yosys-smtbmc, ABC
  PDR. Each run capped at 1800 s (DEC-011 D4).
- **Results in one line.** `formal PASS: 18/18 runs as expected`. The status page
  (`docs/vplan_status.md`) shows SO-3 met.
- **Audit.** A01 and A05 are necessary (spec assertions fail without them, F06 and F07 among
  them). **A02 is not needed by any property** (k-induction passes without it). It stays a
  physical requirement (metastability, plan R11); the formal retirement is DEC-012, proposed.
- **Invariants are needed and hide nothing.** Without them k-induction fails on
  `ast_F06_wfull_not_optimistic`. PDR proves every spec property without them at ASIZE=2 and
  times out at ASIZE=3.
- **Stretch, ASIZE=4** (separate runs, `OUT=out/async_fifo_a4`, overlapping the sign-off):
  `prove` PASS 6.2 s; `cover` 22/22 in 106.9 s (`SBY_OPT="cover: depth 180"`); `bmc` PASS
  76.5 s; `pdr` TIMEOUT at 1800 s.
- **Planted-bug check (preview of M8).** B05 (`rempty_next` from the current read pointer):
  `bmc_spec` FAIL on `ast_F14_rdata_stable_gclk` at step 11 (2.4 s); `prove` UNKNOWN (does not
  pass).
- **Simulation after the M7 property changes** (`$past` → named history registers, FORMAL
  sections): `make sva-neg` 25/25 cases and 8/8 twins; SO-2 rerun `make regress RECHECK=3`
  1,600/1,600 PASS, 150/150 bins, 15/15 covers, 3/3 reruns identical.
